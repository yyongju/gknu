Courses

Since starting at ANU in 2018, I have designed and taught several courses for undergraduate and graduate students.
Here you can find course syllabi, descriptions.


Informatics Subject Pedagogy (3-3-0)

syllabus: Korean, English
offered annually 2018-present

In this course, students understand the concept of informatics subject pedagogy, and systematically learn about the educational goals, learning contents, teaching materials, teaching and learning methods, and evaluation of informatics curriculum in the national curriculum. In addition, students are guided to understand computer science education and form a teaching philosophy for fostering computational thinking skills that are emphasized in computer science education. In addition, they aim for student engagement and agengy and process-oriented evaluation pursued in the national curriculum, and utilize various teaching and learning methods such as cooperative learning, discussion, and project learning so that students can experience them through lectures, and apply activities related to cases in the field, portfolio and observation evaluation of performance tasks during the course of lectures.


Informatics Subject Instructional Methods (3-3-0)

syllabus: Korean, English
offered annually 2018-present

This coures is a compulsory course related to informatics subject pedagogy, and deals with learning theory, teaching and learning methods, evaluation methods, and design of teaching and learning process accordingly. Therefore, this course explores how to design and apply teaching-learning/evaluation methods that can be applied specifically to informatics subject and cross-curricular subjects. In addition, through this course, preliminary teachers' teaching and learning/evaluation skills are developed. The main teaching-learning method is applied to Team Project-Based Learning, and class periods are largely divided into three sessions. In addition, informatics on the middle and high school of the 2015 revised national curriculum presented for each session and the subject matters corresponding to the four instructional areas are freely selected for each team. Lesson plans are designed in a forward and backward manner by applying teaching and learning methods and evaluation methods suitable for each area.


Informatics Subject Logics and Essays (3-3-0)

syllabus: Korean, English
offered annually 2018-present

This course is a compulsory course related to informatics subject pedagogy, and learns about essay writing about subject pedagogy. Therefore, it aims to improve critical and creative thinking ability and self-directed learning ability, not simple memorization ability through repeated learning. Based on this purpose, we present problems that apply computer science content knowledge to subject pedagogy, and in the process of solving them, we conduct education for creative solution methods and logical discourse techniques. - Content knowledge of basic courses of computer education - Curriculum pedagogical knowledge, skills, and attitudes to plan and implement informatics lessons In addition, the 2022 revised national curriculum aims for student engaged classes and process-centered assessments, and utilizes various teaching and learning methods such as problem development, problem-oriented learning, discussion, and programming practice so that students can experience them through lectures. Activities related to cases, portfolios for performance tasks during the course of lectures, observation evaluation, etc. are applied.


Informatics Subject Materials Research and Teaching Practice (3-3-0)

syllabus: Korean, English
offered annually 2018-present

This course consists of content to cultivate practical capabilities for effective informatics instructions through class design, production of class materials, demonstration of mock classes, and class criticism in order to carry out informatics subject classes in the actual field. In addition, by analyzing textbooks of informatics subjects from the viewpoint of the national curriculum and thinking about ways to apply them correctly to class design, it provides experience of using textbooks from the viewpoint of curriculum reconstruction. Students taking this course apply the learning contents of this course through connection with field practice (educational practice), and based on the result of the application, they can improve their informatics education guidance capacity.


Informatics Subject Evaluation Methods (3-3-0)

syllabus: Korean, English
offered annually 2019-present

This class aims at preliminary teachers who have completed all classes of Informatics subject matter education, and summarizes general information on the Informatics subject matter education and strengthens their teaching skills as a preliminary teacher. In particular, students will discuss the national curriculum and textbooks from a variety of perspectives and analyze the teaching, learning and evaluation materials to foster creative curriculum guidance.
