2022 Revised Informatics National Curriculum in South Korea


Informatics - High School (General Elective Subject)

English translation of the Ministry of Education Notice No. 2022-33 [Annex 10], Practical Arts (Technology·Home Economics) / Informatics Curriculum.

1. The Nature and Objectives


A. The Nature of the Subject

'Informatics' is a subject that develops the ability and attitude to recognize the changes in the digital world brought about by data and information in a society defined by artificial intelligence, to explore the social value of information, and to solve problems in real life and in various academic fields on the basis of computational thinking grounded in the diverse principles and technologies of information processing. Responding to national and social demands in the era of digital transformation, 'Informatics' provides the essential competencies that members of society must possess in order to solve problems through computing. The academic foundation of 'Informatics' encompasses the principles of data and information processed by computers, the technologies and methods for designing and implementing computing systems, and an understanding of the human society that deals with information. In other words, on the basis of the basic concepts and principles of informatics — which covers not only computer science but also data science, artificial intelligence, information technology, information systems, and software engineering — 'Informatics' cultivates the knowledge and skills that help solve problems in various academic fields and in future society. These characteristics of the subject help to nurture talented people who possess a basic grounding in the software and artificial intelligence required in every sector of society, and who creatively solve problems not only in engineering but also in diverse academic fields such as the natural sciences, the humanities and social sciences, and the arts and physical education.

High school 'Informatics' enables students to acquire basic competency and self-direction in software and artificial intelligence on the basis of an understanding of data as required by future society. Premised on problem solving through computing, and grounded in the computational thinking by which problems are discovered, analyzed, and solved, it enables students to grow into digital citizens equipped with knowledge and information processing, creative thinking, collaborative communication that allows them to cooperate and share with others, and community competency. In connection with middle school 'Informatics', it strengthens the thinking skills needed to solve the problems of an uncertain future society, and cultivates the abilities and attitudes that help students grow by exploring career paths in fields where the content of the informatics subject is needed. In connection with career elective subjects, it has students take part in developing an overall understanding of artificial intelligence technology, in analyzing data that drives social change and understanding its value, and in software projects that contribute to making a better society, so that they practice the values of collaboration and sharing.

B. Objectives

High school 'Informatics' focuses on cultivating the information-related abilities needed to live independently in a future society in which students will live together with artificial intelligence, so that they can develop computational thinking on the basis of the specialized knowledge of the informatics subject — such as computing devices, information processing, and artificial intelligence — that is required in various academic fields and in real life.

(1) Grasp the principles of connection among the computing systems that link the digital world, and develop the ability to control systems so that the results processed and generated by systems handling information are shared.
(2) Develop the ability to collect data suited to the purpose for problem solving through computing, to grasp and structure the relationships among data, and to process and visualize big data.
(3) Develop the attitude of practicing a culture of collaboration and sharing in the process of modelling the relationships among the data required for problem solving in various academic fields, efficiently designing algorithms, and implementing, evaluating, and improving them as programs.
(4) Understand artificial intelligence from the perspective of the intelligent agent, and develop the ability to master and apply methods of solving problems with artificial intelligence through machine learning.
(5) Understand the development and change of society brought about by digital technology, and develop the attitude and ability to recognize and practice the importance of information protection and information security.

2. Contents and Achievement Standards


A. Contents of Five Domains

(1) Computing Systems
Big Ideas
  • Computing systems that operate through the organic connection of hardware and software are utilized to raise social and technological value
  • Networks make it possible to grasp the principles of connection among multiple computing systems and to share data through communication
Categories Content Elements
Knowledges & Understandings
  • Composition of networks
  • Composition and operating principles of Internet of Things systems
Process & Practices
  • Construct networks among computing systems and configure sharing settings
  • Design Internet of Things systems suited to problem solving
Values & Attitudes
  • A posture of actively utilizing the network environment for collaborative communication
  • An active attitude in coping with the social changes brought about by Internet of Things systems
(2) Data
Big Ideas
  • Compression and encryption of data help to manage and protect data efficiently
  • Grasping and structuring the relationships among collected data helps to find new knowledge through data
  • Correct results are derived only when ethical issues are taken into account in the process of collecting, processing, and managing data using big data technology
Categories Content Elements
Knowledges & Understandings
  • Digital data compression and encryption
  • Concepts and analysis of big data
Process & Practices
  • Analyze and evaluate the efficiency of digital data compression
  • Explore cases in which encryption is used
  • Analyze and visualize data using big data technology
Values & Attitudes
  • A posture of making use of the positive aspects of efficient data representation
  • An attitude of managing and protecting data safely
  • Reflection on the social and ethical aspects of the value of big data analysis
(3) Algorithm & Programming
Big Ideas
  • To solve a problem efficiently, the problem is abstracted and an algorithm for programming is designed
  • To carry out data modelling, the relationships among the data required for problem solving are analyzed and defined
  • Automation through programming helps to solve problems in various academic fields
Categories Content Elements
Knowledges & Understandings
  • Problem decomposition and modelling
  • Sorting and searching algorithms
  • Data types
  • Standard input/output and file input/output
  • Use of multidimensional data
  • Applications of control structures
  • Classes and instances
Process & Practices
  • Decompose and model problems
  • Compare and analyze the execution processes and efficiency of algorithms
  • Write programs using data types and input/output structures suited to problem solving
  • Combine control structures and multidimensional data structures to solve complex problems
  • Define classes and create instances to implement objects suited to problem solving
Values & Attitudes
  • A posture of constructing problem-solving models and expressing them actively
  • An attitude of recognizing the value and influence of algorithmic efficiency and exploring it actively
  • A practical posture of implementing designed algorithms as programs in order to solve problems in various academic fields
  • A posture of recognizing the importance of collaborative problem-solving ability as a democratic citizen of a digital society
(4) Artificial Intelligence
Big Ideas
  • Intelligent agents are utilized, through interaction with the outside world, to solve complex and difficult problems that could not previously be solved
  • To implement machine-learning-based artificial intelligence, data and machine learning models suited to the problem are utilized
Categories Content Elements
Knowledges & Understandings
  • The role of the intelligent agent
  • Concepts and types of machine learning
Process & Practices
  • Explore the role of intelligent agents in artificial intelligence products or services
  • Compare the types of problems that can be solved by machine learning
Values & Attitudes
  • A correct understanding of the relationship between humans and artificial intelligence
  • A posture of actively utilizing machine learning to solve social problems
(5) Digital Culture
Big Ideas
  • Because occupations and career paths in the digital world change as technology advances, it is important to grasp the relationship between technology and social change
  • To live safely in a digital society, all of us must observe the rules of information protection and information security
Categories Content Elements
Knowledges & Understandings
  • Digital society and career paths
  • Information protection and security
Process & Practices
  • Design a career path in connection with the social changes that accompany the advance of digital technology
  • Discover problems that require the application of information protection and security technologies and apply methods of solving them
Values & Attitudes
  • A posture of predicting and gaining insight into the direction of development of future society
  • A correct consciousness of information protection and security


B. Achievement Standards of Five Domains

(1) Computing Systems

[12Inf.01-01] Understand the characteristics of wired and wireless networks, and construct a network environment for sharing, collaboration, and communication among computing systems.
[12Inf.01-02] Analyze the composition and operating principles of the Internet of Things, and predict the changes in individual lives and in society brought about by Internet of Things technology.
[12Inf.01-03] Select physical computing system devices suited to problem solving and design an Internet of Things system.

(2) Data
[12Inf.02-01] Understand the concept of and need for digital data compression, and analyze and evaluate the efficiency of compression.
[12Inf.02-02] Understand the concept of encryption, and compare and analyze cases of managing data safely by using encryption.
[12Inf.02-03] On the basis of an understanding of the concept and characteristics of big data, collect data suited to problem solving.
[12Inf.02-04] Visualize data using big data analysis tools and interpret their meaning and value.

(3) Algorithm & Programming
[12Inf.03-01] Decompose a complex problem into smaller solvable problems and model them.
[12Inf.03-02] Compare and analyze the characteristics and efficiency of various algorithms for sorting data.
[12Inf.03-03] Compare and analyze the characteristics and efficiency of various algorithms for searching data.
[12Inf.03-04] Know the kinds and characteristics of data types, and write programs by selecting appropriate data types.
[12Inf.03-05] Write programs that make use of standard input/output and file input/output.
[12Inf.03-06] Write programs that make use of multidimensional data structures.
[12Inf.03-07] Write programs that combine various control structures.
[12Inf.03-08] Write programs using classes and instances that implement objects.
[12Inf.03-09] Collaboratively design and implement programs to solve problems in real life and in various academic fields.
[12Inf.03-10] Evaluate and share the performance of programs written for problem solving.

(4) Artificial Intelligence
[12Inf.04-01] Understand the concept and characteristics of the intelligent agent, and analyze the relationship between humans and artificial intelligence.
[12Inf.04-02] Understand the concept of machine learning, and compare and analyze the differences between supervised and unsupervised learning.
[12Inf.04-03] Distinguish problems that can be solved by machine learning from those that cannot, and apply machine learning to solving social problems.

(5) Digital Culture
[12Inf.05-01] Analyze the influence of digital technology on society, predict its direction of development, and design a career path.
[12Inf.05-02] Distinguish information that must be protected from information that should be shared, and practice correct methods of information protection.
[12Inf.05-03] Understand the need for information security, and practice digital ethics by making use of security technologies.

3. Teaching/Learning & Evaluations


A. Teaching and Learning

A.1 Directions of Teaching and Learning

(a) Present learning tasks that require learners to solve problems through computing in the contexts of real life, so that learners naturally cultivate computational thinking, digital culture literacy, and artificial intelligence literacy in the process of solving the tasks on their own.
(b) Construct guidance plans for deep learning that cultivates competencies through learner-customized teaching and learning — for example, by providing opportunities for learners to make choices about learning materials, the learning environment, and the learning process in consideration of learners' interests and diversity, and by increasing opportunities for learner participation in the design of teaching and learning.
(c) Using the knowledge and understanding and the processes and skills of the informatics subject, present cross-curricular themes such as sustainable development, together with the various social problems facing the present era such as democratic citizenship education and ecological transition education, as teaching and learning tasks, thereby providing experiences of problem solving with agency.
(d) The order in which the content domains are arranged is illustrative in character; the curriculum is to be reorganized autonomously in consideration of the level of students who have completed middle school, the learning environment of the school, and so on.
(e) Make use of digital-based learning records from online learning platforms so that learning can be extended anytime and anywhere, and organize activities so that competency in collaborative problem solving is cultivated through network-based online activities.

A.2 Methods of Teaching and Learning

(a) In order to cultivate informatics subject competencies, select and utilize teaching and learning methods appropriate for acquiring the big ideas of each domain, such as problem-based learning, project-based learning, design-based learning, pair programming, and inquiry learning.
(b) Take into account that learners may learn at widely differing paces, and endeavour to minimize learning gaps by producing and providing online learning materials through a learning management system (LMS) so that a minimum level of achievement can be guaranteed.
(c) Provide meaningful learner-centred activity experiences by utilizing problem-based learning, in which problems presented through cross-domain curriculum reorganization are solved, and project-based learning, in which learners select and investigate a topic.
(d) So that students can adapt to the digital education environment, organize lessons through online-offline linked classes and the use of various digital tools, so as to minimize the cognitive burden of digital tools while increasing experience in using them.
(e) In programming, place the focus on the process of solving problems rather than on memorizing and acquiring the language; select real-life or subject-related topics that learners will find interesting, present them as tasks, and organize teaching and learning so that learners solve them on their own.

B. Evaluation

B.1 Directions of Evaluation

(a) Assessment items are specified on the basis of the sub-factors of computational thinking, digital culture literacy, and artificial intelligence literacy.
(b) Assessment content reflects in multiple dimensions not only knowledge and understanding but also the aspects of processes and skills and of values and attitudes, and realizes assessment that helps students grow and develop through process-oriented assessment.
(c) Through the process of constructing specific assessment rubrics together with students, provide opportunities for students to grasp their own level of learning and reflect on their learning for themselves, so that active and self-directed learning takes place.
(d) In addition to assessment aimed at grasping the level of attainment of the achievement standards, present tasks in which learned content can be applied through transfer, thereby assessing understanding and thinking in an integrated manner.

B.2 Methods of Evaluation

(a) Analyze and reorganize the achievement standards to plan an assessment portfolio that is not limited to written tests and that can contribute to students' growth. For example, utilize observational assessment, descriptive assessment, and performance assessment, or implement multi-faceted assessment such as self-assessment and peer assessment.
(b) Depending on the assessment content and method, various digital tools may be used (automated program assessment systems such as online judges, learning management systems (LMS), and the like); plan to train students to handle the digital tools before the assessment so that they suffer no disadvantage in the assessment.
(c) Have students discover for themselves problems that can be solved in real life and in various academic fields, and accumulate the performance processes by which students solve them in the form of reports or portfolios, so that assessment is continuous and focused on process.
(d) Present assessment criteria subdivided by level of difficulty so that students who are struggling or learning slowly can participate and so that a minimum level of achievement can be guaranteed for learners.
(e) Cultivate digital literacy through process assessment that uses computing systems — collecting, processing, and analyzing data with software suited to the problem.
