2022 Revised Informatics National Curriculum in South Korea


Artificial Intelligence Basics - High School (Career Elective Subject)

English translation of the Ministry of Education Notice No. 2022-33 [Annex 10], Practical Arts (Technology·Home Economics) / Informatics Curriculum. Achievement standard codes appear in the original as [12인기xx-xx]; they are rendered here as [12AI.xx-xx].

1. The Nature and Objectives


A. The Nature of the Subject

'Informatics' is a subject that develops the ability and attitude to recognize the changes in the digital world brought about by data and information in a society defined by artificial intelligence, to explore the social value of information, and to solve problems in real life and in various academic fields on the basis of computational thinking grounded in the diverse principles and technologies of information processing. Responding to national and social demands in the era of digital transformation, 'Informatics' provides the essential competencies that members of society must possess in order to solve problems through computing. The academic foundation of 'Informatics' encompasses the principles of data and information processed by computers, the technologies and methods for designing and implementing computing systems, and an understanding of the human society that deals with information. In other words, on the basis of the basic concepts and principles of informatics — which covers not only computer science but also data science, artificial intelligence, information technology, information systems, and software engineering — 'Informatics' cultivates the knowledge and skills that help solve problems in various academic fields and in future society. These characteristics of the subject help to nurture talented people who possess a basic grounding in the software and artificial intelligence required in every sector of society, and who creatively solve problems not only in engineering but also in diverse academic fields such as the natural sciences, the humanities and social sciences, and the arts and physical education.

'Artificial Intelligence Basics', grounded in the content of computer science, data science, and information systems among the academic disciplines, provides a deep understanding of the artificial intelligence that is exerting a direct influence on individual lives and on a wide range of fields. It enables students to respond actively to the changes in careers and occupations brought about by the change and uncertainty of future society, and enables learners — as autonomous users of artificial intelligence — to grow into digital democratic citizens with the self-direction to use artificial intelligence safely and responsibly in a human-centred way. On the basis of in-depth learning about artificial intelligence — including direct implementation through project-based programming and the evaluation of models — it provides foundational experience connected to university majors.

B. Objectives

'Artificial Intelligence Basics' focuses on grasping the social changes that accompany the development of artificial intelligence, on being able to program artificial intelligence as a core tool for creatively solving problems in various fields on the basis of an understanding of its principles, and on cultivating sound values and attitudes concerning the ethical issues of artificial intelligence.

(1) On the basis of an understanding of artificial intelligence, develop the ability and attitude to grasp real-life problems from the perspective of artificial intelligence and to apply methods of search and inference in order to implement intelligent judgement.
(2) Define problems that can be solved by means of machine learning, and develop the ability and attitude to solve problems effectively by making use of the data and models required in the problem-solving process.
(3) Explore the changes in human life and careers that accompany the development of artificial intelligence, and develop the attitude of forming sound values concerning the ethical problems related to artificial intelligence on the basis of a critical posture toward its various aspects.
(4) Recognize that artificial intelligence can create new value through convergence with various fields, and develop the ability and attitude to solve the problems humanity faces by making use of artificial intelligence.

2. Contents and Achievement Standards


A. Contents of Four Domains

(1) Understanding Artificial Intelligence
Big Ideas
  • Artificial intelligence imitates intelligent human behaviour and thereby helps in real life
  • Implementing artificial intelligence that solves problems by search and inference is applied in various academic fields

  • Principles of artificial intelligence
  • Artificial intelligence and search
  • Knowledge representation and inference
Process & Practices
  • Explore cases of problem solving based on artificial intelligence
  • Apply search algorithms to problem solving
  • Explore methods of generating new knowledge through inference
Values & Attitudes
  • Recognition of the necessity and applicability of artificial intelligence
  • A posture of solving problems in real life and in various academic fields by making use of artificial intelligence
(2) Artificial Intelligence and Learning
Big Ideas
  • To implement machine-learning-based artificial intelligence, data and machine learning models suited to the problem are utilized
  • Deep learning, as an artificial neural network composed of multiple hidden layers, is utilized to solve complex problems effectively

  • Machine learning and data
  • Machine learning algorithms
  • Artificial neural networks and deep learning
Process & Practices
  • Define problems to which machine learning is to be applied

  • Select machine learning algorithms suited to the problem and implement models
  • Explore and implement methods of problem solving that make use of deep learning

  • Judgement of the importance of the data applied in machine learning
  • Recognition of the effectiveness and efficiency of artificial intelligence through learning
(3) Social Impact of Artificial Intelligence

  • The influence of artificial intelligence on contemporary society is growing, and with it the pace of change in occupations is accelerating
  • To make use of artificial intelligence properly, an understanding of the ethical problems it may give rise to is needed

  • Recognition of the importance of artificial intelligence from the perspective of careers and occupations
  • A critical posture and an ethical attitude toward the various aspects of artificial intelligence

  • Artificial intelligence helps to create new value through convergence with various fields
  • Artificial intelligence helps to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals

B. Achievement Standards of Four Domains

(1) Understanding Artificial Intelligence

[12AI.01-01] On the basis of an understanding of the intelligent judgement of artificial intelligence, compare and analyze cases of problem solving in real life and in various academic fields that make use of artificial intelligence.
[12AI.01-02] Understand the importance of search in artificial intelligence and design a search process for problem solving.
[12AI.01-03] Grasp the principles of intelligent search, centring on the difference between blind search and informed search.
[12AI.01-04] Find problems that require intelligent search and apply informed search algorithms to solving them.
[12AI.01-05] Represent knowledge using rules and facts, and generate new knowledge by inference.

(2) Artificial Intelligence and Learning
[12AI.02-01] Define a problem to which machine learning is to be applied, and select and collect the data required for solving it.
[12AI.02-02] Process the collected data and extract the key attributes.
[12AI.02-03] Select the type of machine learning and the algorithm suited to solving the problem.
[12AI.02-04] Carry out learning using training data, and evaluate performance using test data.
[12AI.02-05] On the basis of an understanding of the characteristics of artificial neural networks and deep learning, explore fields of application.
[12AI.02-06] Solve problems in real life and in various academic fields by making use of deep learning, and evaluate the performance.

(3) Social Impact of Artificial Intelligence
[12AI.03-01] Examine the social changes brought about by the development of artificial intelligence, and analyze social problems that can be solved with artificial intelligence.
[12AI.03-02] Understand the ways in which human life and occupations are changed by artificial intelligence, and explore career paths.
[12AI.03-03] On the basis of a critical posture toward artificial intelligence, derive ways for artificial intelligence and humans to coexist.
[12AI.03-04] Analyze cases in which artificial intelligence is used and situations of ethical dilemma from the perspective of artificial intelligence ethics.

(4) Artificial Intelligence Projects
[12AI.04-01] Explore ways in which artificial intelligence can be applied to addressing the Sustainable Development Goals, and derive topics suited to artificial intelligence project activities.
[12AI.04-02] Devise a project execution plan on the basis of the process of problem solving with artificial intelligence.
[12AI.04-03] Develop artificial intelligence software on the basis of a collaborative problem-solving posture in the course of carrying out an artificial intelligence project.
[12AI.04-04] Develop artificial intelligence software in consideration of the social impact of artificial intelligence, and improve its performance by reflecting the results of evaluation.

3. Teaching/Learning & Evaluations


A. Teaching and Learning

A.1 Directions of Teaching and Learning

(a) Present learning tasks that require learners to solve problems through computing in the contexts of real life, so that learners naturally cultivate computational thinking, digital culture literacy, and artificial intelligence literacy in the process of solving the tasks on their own.
(b) Construct guidance plans for deep learning that cultivates competencies through learner-customized teaching and learning — for example, by providing opportunities for learners to make choices about learning materials, the learning environment, and the learning process in consideration of learners' interests and diversity, and by increasing opportunities for learner participation in the design of teaching and learning.
(c) Using the knowledge and understanding and the processes and skills of the Artificial Intelligence Basics subject, present cross-curricular themes such as sustainable development, together with the various social problems facing the present era such as democratic citizenship education and ecological transition education, as teaching and learning tasks, thereby providing experiences of problem solving with agency.
(d) Teaching and learning in 'Artificial Intelligence Basics' focuses on improving the competency to solve problems efficiently on the basis of an understanding of the principles of artificial intelligence. Through convergence among the content domains within the subject and with other subjects and non-curricular activities, students' artificial intelligence literacy should be transferable to a variety of fields.

A.2 Methods of Teaching and Learning

(a) In order to cultivate subject competencies, select and utilize teaching and learning methods appropriate for acquiring the big ideas of each domain, such as problem-based learning, project-based learning, design-based learning, pair programming, and inquiry learning.
(b) Take into account that learners may learn at widely differing paces, and endeavour to minimize learning gaps by producing and providing online learning materials through a learning management system (LMS) so that a minimum level of achievement can be guaranteed.
(c) Grasp learners' digital and artificial intelligence literacy in advance and organize teaching and learning activities in a way suited to students' current levels. Centre the work on the development of artificial intelligence programs through programming, while actively making use of software and artificial-intelligence-related libraries that help in understanding artificial intelligence concepts, so that a minimum level of achievement is guaranteed.
(d) So that students can adapt to the digital education environment, organize lessons through online-offline linked classes and the use of various digital tools, so as to minimize the cognitive burden of digital tools while increasing experience in using them.
(e) Organize the teaching and learning process so as to introduce the careers and occupations connected to the 'Artificial Intelligence Basics' subject and to allow students to explore their own career paths.

B. Evaluation

B.1 Directions of Evaluation

(a) Assessment items are specified on the basis of the sub-factors of computational thinking, digital culture literacy, and artificial intelligence literacy.
(b) Assessment content reflects in multiple dimensions not only knowledge and understanding but also the aspects of processes and skills and of values and attitudes, and realizes assessment that helps students grow and develop through process-oriented assessment.
(c) Through the process of constructing specific assessment rubrics together with students, provide opportunities for students to grasp their own level of learning and reflect on their learning for themselves, so that active and self-directed learning takes place.
(d) Although programming activities are included in the implementation of artificial intelligence programs, assess with the focus on the implementation and use of artificial intelligence and on awareness of its use, rather than on the programming content.

B.2 Methods of Evaluation

(a) Analyze and reorganize the achievement standards to plan a comprehensive assessment portfolio that makes use of written tests, performance assessment, observational assessment, and descriptive and essay-type assessment, and implement multi-faceted assessment such as teacher assessment, self-assessment, and peer assessment, so as to provide results that can contribute to students' growth.
(b) Depending on the assessment content and method, various digital tools may be used (automated program assessment systems such as online judges, learning management systems (LMS), and the like); plan to train students to handle the digital tools before the assessment so that they suffer no disadvantage in the assessment.
(c) Because assessment related to artificial intelligence can take a variety of forms, clearly distinguish quantitative from qualitative assessment content. In particular, for projects in which model training and application are carried out repeatedly, plan the assessment so that the results are fed back to students' activities immediately and appear as improvements in the product.
